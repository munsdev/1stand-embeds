Tarrant County polling sites, 2022 to 2026

Every Election Day and early voting site, with what happened to it and where it is. 433 records, 431 geocoded. Read the findings.

Files

Workbook (.xlsx) Nine tabs: every site, the closures, additions, retained, renamed, ZIP and city summaries, early voting, and full method notes. Closures (.csv) The 149 closed and 56 added Election Day sites with addresses and coordinates. Coordinates (.json) Raw geocoding output, keyed by site name and address. Report (.pdf) Four page printable version with maps.

Use the map elsewhere

The interactive map is a self-contained page with no external dependencies beyond OpenStreetMap tiles. It is free to embed on any site. Open it on its own, or paste this: 

<iframe
  src="https://munsdev.github.io/1stand-embeds/tarrant-polling/standalone.html"
  style="width:100%;height:80vh;border:0"
  title="Tarrant County polling sites, 2022 to 2026"
  loading="lazy" allowfullscreen></iframe>
